Questions we get asked

What do I need to send for a quote?

Your drawing or a sample, the material and thickness, the quantity and the date you need it by. Laser jobs accept DWG, DXF, STEP, IGES and PDF.

What is the smallest order?

Dies and tooling start at one piece. Power press runs start at 1,000 pieces. Larger runs of clamps and pressed parts are quoted per piece.

Which materials can you cut?

Mild steel, stainless steel, aluminium, copper, carbide and high-speed steel on wire EDM. Mild steel up to 15 mm on the laser.

Can you make a progressive die for my part?

Yes. We build 6 and 10 station progressive tools for piercing, forming and embossing. Send the part drawing and your press tonnage.

Do you make tablet punches and dies?

Yes, in stainless steel, case hardened, for pharmaceutical tablet presses. The minimum order is 10 sets.
